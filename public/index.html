RackSight

REQUIRED REVIEW

Notifications and monitoring only, by design

RackSight monitors datacenter hardware and alerts you when attention is required. Monitoring does not resolve a failure.

What RackSight does

Reads Redfish monitoring data, retains telemetry history, and sends encrypted server, fan, temperature, and recovery alerts to enrolled Android devices.

What Android cannot do

The Android app cannot send BMC, host, console, reset, remediation, configuration, or power commands back to the datacenter. It has no remote console or host access.
